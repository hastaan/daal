A two-path onboarding

The first ninety seconds, two ways.

People inside Iran land on the recipient flow — get a route, press connect. Diaspora who want to help family land on the publisher flow — set up a server, share a subscription with the people they love. Both paths leave first-launch ready.

Your routes stay yours.

They live on this device. Daal does not see your traffic, and we do not run a server.

9:41
Publisher mode

Welcome, publisher.

You'll create routes that family and friends can subscribe to. Your server, your keys, your trusted circle — Daal does not see any of it.

i.

You sign each route.

Subscribers verify your fingerprint once. They trust the publisher, not the network.

ii.

You stay anonymous to Daal.

There is no Daal server. Your subscription URL goes peer-to-peer, or through whatever channel you choose.

Both paths converge.

From here, both users land on the main app's Connection screen. The recipient sees the phoenix glowing, one tap from connected. The publisher sees a small "publisher dashboard" entry alongside, where they can manage subscribers, rotate keys, and watch route health.

The branching choice is reversible: Settings → Advanced → "I am a publisher" toggles the publisher surface on for any user who started as a recipient.
